Issue-interest layer

Core policy interest: cryptocurrency and blockchain regulation, including federal market-structure and stablecoin legislation. The network is deliberately bipartisan: donor funds enter Fairshake, then can be routed to a Republican-facing or Democratic-facing affiliate. Candidate spending is an independent expenditure and is not a contribution to the candidate.
Interpretation constraint: A donor → PAC → candidate path establishes disclosed financial support or opposition. It does not establish that the recipient changed a vote or policy position because of that support. Policy outcomes should be shown as a separate evidence layer.
